## Sections C-F

These are productive tasks. Check them using this rule summary:

- **Regular -re verbs:** Remove -re and add -s, -s, nothing, -ons, -ez, -ent. The final consonant is often heard in plural forms but not singular forms.
- **Depuis:** Use depuis with the present tense for something that began in the past and is still continuing.
- **Spelling changes:** Some -er verbs change spelling to preserve sound or stress: commençons, mangeons, j'achète, je préfère, j'appelle, j'essaie.
- **Shopping language:** Apply verb forms to clothing, toiletries, preferences, and time expressions.

## Self-scoring

A corrected answer is not mastered until you can produce a different correct example on a later day.
