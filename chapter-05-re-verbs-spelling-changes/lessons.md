# Chapter 5 Lessons: Regular -re Verbs and -er Spelling Changes

Use short sessions. Accuracy from memory matters more than copying volume.

## Unit 1: Regular -re verbs

Remove -re and add -s, -s, nothing, -ons, -ez, -ent. The final consonant is often heard in plural forms but not singular forms.

### Examples

- `J'attends le bus.` — I am waiting for the bus.
- `Nous vendons des livres.` — We sell books.
- `Ils répondent vite.` — They answer quickly.

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 2: Depuis

Use depuis with the present tense for something that began in the past and is still continuing.

### Examples

- `J'habite ici depuis deux ans.` — I have lived here for two years.
- `Elle attend depuis midi.` — She has been waiting since noon.
- `Depuis quand travaillez-vous ici ?` — How long have you worked here?

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 3: Spelling changes

Some -er verbs change spelling to preserve sound or stress: commençons, mangeons, j'achète, je préfère, j'appelle, j'essaie.

### Examples

- `Nous commençons.` — We are beginning.
- `Nous mangeons.` — We are eating.
- `Elle achète une veste.` — She buys a jacket.

## Chapter production task

Write a short store dialogue using one -re verb, three spelling-change verbs, a color, and depuis.

After writing, circle conjugated verbs, underline agreement or pronoun choices, and read the passage aloud twice.

## Sentence usage practice

Use these complete sentences to practice the chapter structures in context. Read each sentence aloud twice, cover it, and write it from memory.

1. `J'attends mon ami devant le magasin.` — I am waiting for my friend in front of the store.
2. `Nous habitons ici depuis trois ans.` — We have lived here for three years.
3. `Elle achète une chemise et essaie une veste.` — She buys a shirt and tries on a jacket.
4. `Nous mangeons au restaurant avant de commencer les achats.` — We eat at the restaurant before starting the shopping.

### Notice and mark

For each sentence, circle the target verb or grammar structure. Underline the word or phrase that controls its form.

### Change the sentence

Rewrite every sentence twice:

1. change the subject, person, or number;
2. change one meaningful detail such as the place, object, time, or description.

Make every required change to conjugation, agreement, articles, pronouns, and word order.

### Make it personal

Write four new sentences about your own life using the same four chapter structures. Read them aloud, check them, and rewrite only the parts you corrected.
