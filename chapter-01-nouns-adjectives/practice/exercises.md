# Chapter 1 Exercises

Do these without notes. Mark uncertain answers with `?` rather than guessing silently.

## A. Choose the definite article

Fill in `le`, `la`, `l'`, or `les`.

1. ___ restaurant
2. ___ gare
3. ___ école
4. ___ hôtels
5. ___ problème
6. ___ solution
7. ___ arbres
8. ___ histoire

:::answer
1. `le` — `restaurant` is masculine singular.
2. `la` — `gare` is feminine singular.
3. `l'` — `école` begins with a vowel sound, so `la` contracts.
4. `les` — `hôtels` is plural, even though the **h** is silent.
5. `le` — `problème` is masculine despite ending in **-e**.
6. `la` — `solution` is feminine.
7. `les` — `arbres` is plural.
8. `l'` — `histoire` begins with a silent **h**, so `la` contracts.

The trap is `l'`: it hides gender. You still need to learn whether the noun is masculine or feminine.
:::


## B. Choose the indefinite article

Fill in `un`, `une`, or `des`.

1. ___ exercice
2. ___ question
3. ___ amis
4. ___ téléphone
5. ___ adresse
6. ___ voitures

:::answer
1. `un` — `exercice` is masculine singular.
2. `une` — `question` is feminine singular.
3. `des` — `amis` is plural.
4. `un` — `téléphone` is masculine singular.
5. `une` — `adresse` is feminine singular.
6. `des` — `voitures` is plural.

Indefinite articles mean a/an/some. Use `des` for plural, not a plural form of `un` or `une`.
:::


## C. Make the phrase plural

1. `une petite fenêtre`
2. `un tableau moderne`
3. `le nouvel appartement`
4. `la voix française`
5. `un animal heureux`

:::answer
1. `des petites fenêtres` — `une` becomes `des`, and both noun and adjective become plural.
2. `des tableaux modernes` — nouns ending in **-eau** often take **-x** in the plural; the adjective takes **-s**.
3. `les nouveaux appartements` — `nouvel` changes back to `nouveaux` in the masculine plural.
4. `les voix françaises` — `voix` already ends in **x**, so the noun spelling does not change; the adjective becomes plural.
5. `des animaux heureux` — `animal` becomes `animaux`; `heureux` already ends in **x**.

Check the whole noun phrase: article, noun, and adjective. Changing only one part is the most common error.
:::


## D. Make the adjective agree

Use the adjective in parentheses.

1. une fille ___ (`sportif`)
2. des maisons ___ (`blanc`)
3. un homme ___ (`heureux`)
4. des étudiantes ___ (`canadien`)
5. une rue ___ (`calme`)
6. des garçons ___ (`actif`)
7. une ___ voisine (`nouveau`)
8. un ___ hôtel (`vieux`)

:::answer
1. `sportive` — `fille` is feminine singular, so `sportif` becomes `sportive`.
2. `blanches` — `maisons` is feminine plural, so add feminine **-e** and plural **-s**.
3. `heureux` — masculine singular `homme` uses the base form; `heureux` already ends in **x**.
4. `canadiennes` — `étudiantes` is feminine plural, so `canadien` becomes `canadiennes`.
5. `calme` — this adjective already ends in **-e**, so masculine and feminine singular look the same.
6. `actifs` — `garçons` is masculine plural, so add **-s** to `actif`.
7. `nouvelle` — before feminine singular `voisine`, use the feminine form of `nouveau`.
8. `vieil` — masculine singular `hôtel` begins with a silent **h**, so use the special before-vowel form.

The trap is assuming agreement always just adds **-e**. Several adjectives change spelling more deeply.
:::


## E. Put the words in a natural order

1. `rouge / une / voiture`
2. `appartement / un / beau`
3. `difficiles / des / exercices`
4. `ville / grande / une`
5. `françaises / étudiantes / des`

:::answer
1. `une voiture rouge` — most color adjectives follow the noun.
2. `un bel appartement` — `beau` comes before the noun and becomes `bel` before a vowel sound.
3. `des exercices difficiles` — most descriptive adjectives follow the noun and agree in plural.
4. `une grande ville` — common size adjectives such as `grand` often come before the noun and agree.
5. `des étudiantes françaises` — nationality adjectives normally follow the noun and agree.

Position and agreement are separate checks. First put the adjective in the natural place, then make it match the noun.
:::


## F. Correct the mistake

Each phrase contains one error.

1. `le maison`
2. `une garçon`
3. `des voiture`
4. `une sac noire`
5. `un belle jardin`
6. `des étudiantes canadien`
7. `une chambre blancs`
8. `un nouveau ami`

:::answer
1. `la maison` — `maison` is feminine, so `le` is wrong.
2. `un garçon` — `garçon` is masculine, so `une` is wrong.
3. `des voitures` — plural noun and plural article are both required.
4. `un sac noir` — `sac` is masculine, so both article and adjective must be masculine.
5. `un beau jardin` — `jardin` is masculine, so use `un` and `beau`.
6. `des étudiantes canadiennes` — `étudiantes` is feminine plural, so the nationality adjective must be feminine plural.
7. `une chambre blanche` — `chambre` is feminine singular, so the color must be feminine singular.
8. `un nouvel ami` — masculine `ami` begins with a vowel sound, so `nouveau` becomes `nouvel`.

Every error here is an agreement or article error. Correct the whole phrase, not just the word that first looks wrong.
:::


## G. Translate into French

1. a small house
2. the blue books
3. a French student (female)
4. some old neighborhoods
5. the new hotel
6. some happy Canadian women

:::answer
Reasonable answers:

1. `une petite maison` — `maison` is feminine, and `petite` agrees with it.
2. `les livres bleus` — `livres` is masculine plural, so the color is `bleus`.
3. `une étudiante française` — female student requires feminine article and feminine nationality adjective.
4. `de vieux quartiers` — `vieux` comes before the plural noun, so `des` commonly becomes `de`.
5. `le nouvel hôtel` — `hôtel` begins with a silent **h**, so use `nouvel` before it.
6. `des Canadiennes heureuses` — as a noun for people, `Canadiennes` is capitalized; `heureuses` agrees feminine plural.

Item 4 is the advanced-looking trap: before a plural adjective placed before the noun, `des` often becomes `de`.
:::


## H. Guided writing

Write a four-sentence description of an apartment. Include:

- at least four nouns with articles;
- one singular and one plural noun;
- two colors;
- one adjective before a noun;
- two adjectives after nouns.

:::answer
Answers vary. A model four-sentence description:

1. `J'habite dans un petit appartement.`
2. `Il y a une cuisine blanche et un salon moderne.`
3. `Les grandes fenêtres sont près d'une table noire.`
4. `La chambre est calme.`

Self-marking criteria:

- At least four nouns have articles: for example `un appartement`, `une cuisine`, `un salon`, `les fenêtres`.
- There is at least one singular noun and one plural noun.
- Two colors appear and agree, such as `blanche` and `noire`.
- At least one adjective comes before a noun, such as `petit` or `grandes`.
- At least two adjectives come after nouns, such as `blanche`, `moderne`, `noire`, or `calme`.

Mark yourself wrong if an adjective has the right meaning but the wrong gender or number.
:::


## I. Mixed mastery check

Choose the correct option.

1. `___ université` — `le / la / l'`
2. `une femme ___` — `canadien / canadienne`
3. `des ___ maisons` — `grand / grande / grandes`
4. `un ___ immeuble` — `nouveau / nouvel / nouvelle`
5. `des sacs ___` — `noir / noire / noirs`
6. `___ animaux` — `un / une / des`
7. `une leçon ___` — `difficile / difficiles`
8. `Paul et Anne sont ___` — `heureuse / heureux / heureuses`

:::answer
1. `l'` — `université` begins with a vowel sound, so `la` contracts.
2. `canadienne` — `femme` is feminine singular.
3. `grandes` — `maisons` is feminine plural, and `grand` comes before the noun.
4. `nouvel` — masculine `immeuble` begins with a vowel sound.
5. `noirs` — `sacs` is masculine plural.
6. `des` — `animaux` is plural.
7. `difficile` — `leçon` is feminine singular, but `difficile` already ends in **-e**.
8. `heureux` — a mixed group uses masculine plural agreement; `heureux` already ends in **x**.

If you missed an item, name the category: article, plural, adjective agreement, or special before-vowel adjective form.
:::
