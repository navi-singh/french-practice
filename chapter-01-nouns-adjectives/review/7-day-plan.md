# Seven-Day Study Plan

Aim for 30-40 focused minutes per day. Stop before concentration drops.

## Day 1: Gender and definite articles

1. Read Lesson 1.
2. Say every example twice.
3. Complete Writing Drill 1.
4. Complete Exercise A.
5. Record errors.

## Day 2: Indefinite articles and plurals

1. Recall yesterday's four definite articles before opening notes.
2. Read Lesson 2.
3. Complete Writing Drill 3.
4. Complete Exercises B and C.
5. Review only yesterday's errors.

## Day 3: Adjective agreement

1. Write the four forms of `petit` from memory.
2. Read Lesson 3.
3. Complete Writing Drill 2.
4. Complete Exercise D.
5. Read answers aloud after correction.

## Day 4: Position, colors, and nationalities

1. Recall three adjectives that usually precede nouns.
2. Read Lesson 4.
3. Complete Exercises E and F.
4. Do Writing Drill 4 as dictation.
5. Add errors to the log.

## Day 5: Production day

1. Complete Exercise G.
2. Complete Writing Drills 5 and 6.
3. Complete Exercise H.
4. Read your paragraph aloud twice.
5. Correct only after finishing the full paragraph.

## Day 6: Listening and pronunciation

1. Use `audio-script.md` or `tts-narration.txt`.
2. Shadow ten French phrases: listen, pause, and imitate rhythm.
3. Write five heard phrases as dictation.
4. Compare your spelling and agreement.
5. Repeat difficult phrases slowly, then at natural speed.

## Day 7: Closed-book mastery check

1. Complete Exercise I.
2. Redo every item previously entered in the error log.
3. Write five original sentences.
4. Explain two corrections aloud in English.
5. Schedule reviews for Day 10, Day 14, and Day 30.

## Delayed reviews

At each delayed review:

1. Write the article table from memory.
2. Transform two singular phrases into plural.
3. Produce all four forms of one adjective.
4. Write two original descriptive sentences.
5. Redo only errors and uncertain items.
