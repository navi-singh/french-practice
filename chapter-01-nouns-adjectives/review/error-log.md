## Error categories

Use one of these labels:

- noun gender
- definite article
- indefinite article
- noun plural
- adjective gender
- adjective plural
- adjective position
- special adjective form
- spelling/accent
- pronunciation/listening

An error is closed only after you answer a new example correctly from memory on a later day.
