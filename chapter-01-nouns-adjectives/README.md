# Chapter 1 Learning Pack: Nouns, Articles, and Adjectives

This is an original companion course for Chapter 1 of *Easy French Step-by-Step*. It teaches the chapter's concepts without reproducing the book.

## What you will learn

1. Recognize masculine and feminine nouns.
2. Choose `le`, `la`, `l'`, and `les`.
3. Choose `un`, `une`, and `des`.
4. Form common noun plurals.
5. Make descriptive adjectives agree in gender and number.
6. Place common adjectives before or after the noun.
7. Use colors and nationalities accurately.

## Best learning order

Use the materials in this order:

1. Read one short lesson in [`lessons/`](lessons/).
2. Read the side-by-side English meaning, then say every French example aloud.
3. Complete the matching section in [`practice/writing-drills.md`](practice/writing-drills.md) by hand.
4. Close the lesson and answer from memory.
5. Complete the matching exercises in [`practice/exercises.md`](practice/exercises.md).
6. Correct your work with [`practice/answer-key.md`](practice/answer-key.md).
7. Record errors in [`review/error-log.md`](review/error-log.md).
8. Follow the review schedule in [`review/7-day-plan.md`](review/7-day-plan.md).

The existing [`audio-script.md`](audio-script.md), `tts-narration.txt`, and `chapter-01-audio-script.docx` provide listening and pronunciation practice.

## Does writing help you learn French faster?

Yes, especially when writing is combined with **retrieval and correction**. Copying a paragraph repeatedly is less effective than trying to produce a phrase from memory and then checking it.

The most useful cycle is:

> See it -> say it -> cover it -> write it from memory -> check it -> rewrite only the correction

Handwriting can slow you down enough to notice accents, gender, endings, and word order. However, writing alone will not train listening or pronunciation. A balanced beginner session should include:

| Activity | Time |
|---|---:|
| Learn one small rule | 5 minutes |
| Say examples aloud | 5 minutes |
| Write from memory | 10 minutes |
| Complete exercises | 10 minutes |
| Review mistakes | 5 minutes |

## Mastery target

Move to Chapter 2 when you can:

- score at least **85%** on the mixed review without looking at notes;
- explain why an article or adjective form is correct;
- write five original noun phrases with correct agreement;
- read those phrases aloud comfortably.
