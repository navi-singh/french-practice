# Lesson 3: Adjective Agreement

## Core idea

A descriptive adjective normally agrees with the noun it describes.

| Noun form | Typical adjective form |
|---|---|
| masculine singular | base form |
| feminine singular | add `-e` |
| masculine plural | add `-s` |
| feminine plural | add `-es` |

Example with `petit`:

- `un petit appartement` — a small apartment
- `une petite maison` — a small house
- `des petits appartements` — some small apartments
- `des petites maisons` — some small houses

## Common spelling patterns

| Masculine | Feminine | English |
|---|---|---|
| `actif` | `active` | active |
| `heureux` | `heureuse` | happy |
| `cher` | `chère` | expensive/dear |
| `naturel` | `naturelle` | natural |
| `bon` | `bonne` | good |
| `canadien` | `canadienne` | Canadian |

Some adjectives already end in `-e` and do not change in the feminine:

- `un garçon calme` — a calm boy
- `une fille calme` — a calm girl

## Mixed groups

A plural adjective describing a group containing at least one masculine noun traditionally uses the masculine plural:

- `Paul et Marie sont contents.` — Paul and Marie are happy.

## Sentence usage practice

Read the sentences aloud and identify the noun controlled by each adjective.

1. `Paul est content de son résultat.` — Paul is happy with his result.
2. `Marie est contente de son résultat.` — Marie is happy with her result.
3. `Paul et Marc sont contents de leurs résultats.` — Paul and Marc are happy with their results.
4. `Marie et Léa sont contentes de leurs résultats.` — Marie and Léa are happy with their results.
5. `Paul et Marie sont contents de leurs résultats.` — Paul and Marie are happy with their results.
6. `Les étudiantes canadiennes sont très actives.` — The Canadian female students are very active.
7. `Le garçon sportif parle avec une fille sportive.` — The athletic boy speaks with an athletic girl.

### Notice the pattern

Underline `content`, `contente`, `contents`, and `contentes`. Draw an arrow from each adjective to the noun or people it describes.

### Change the sentence

Rewrite sentences 1-5 with `fatigué` and then with `heureux`. Make every gender and number change.

### Make it personal

Write four sentences about people you know:

1. one masculine singular;
2. one feminine singular;
3. one feminine plural;
4. one mixed group.
