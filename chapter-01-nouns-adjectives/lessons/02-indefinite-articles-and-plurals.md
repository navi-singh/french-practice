# Lesson 2: Indefinite Articles and Plurals

## Indefinite articles

Use these forms for “a,” “an,” or plural “some”:

| Noun | Article | French example | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| masculine singular | `un` | `un cahier` | a notebook |
| feminine singular | `une` | `une lampe` | a lamp |
| plural | `des` | `des cahiers`, `des lampes` | some notebooks, some lamps |

Compare:

- `le cahier` — the notebook
- `un cahier` — a notebook
- `les cahiers` — the notebooks
- `des cahiers` — some notebooks

## Forming plurals

The common pattern is to add `-s`:

- `une porte` -> `des portes` — a door -> some doors
- `un village` -> `des villages` — a village -> some villages

Common special patterns:

- Many nouns ending in `-eau` take `-x`: `un tableau` -> `des tableaux` — a painting/board -> some paintings/boards
- Many nouns ending in `-al` change to `-aux`: `un animal` -> `des animaux` — an animal -> some animals
- A noun already ending in `-s`, `-x`, or `-z` often does not change in writing.

Learn exceptions as complete singular/plural pairs.

## Pronunciation note

The final plural `-s` is normally silent. The article often carries the clearest number signal:

- `le livre` — the book
- `les livres` — the books

## Retrieval check

Change each phrase to plural:

1. `une photo`
2. `un bureau`
3. `le journal`
4. `la voix`

## Production task

Write three singular objects you own. Convert each to plural and read both versions aloud.

## Sentence usage practice

Read, copy once, and then reproduce each sentence from memory.

1. `J'ai un cahier bleu dans mon sac.` — I have a blue notebook in my bag.
2. `Elle achète une lampe pour la chambre.` — She buys a lamp for the bedroom.
3. `Nous avons des photos de Paris.` — We have some photos of Paris.
4. `Les bureaux sont près des fenêtres.` — The desks are near the windows.
5. `Il observe des animaux au parc.` — He watches some animals at the park.

### Change the sentence

Change each singular object to plural. Then change each plural object to singular. Remember to change both the article and the noun.

### Make it personal

Write four sentences beginning with `J'ai...`: two with singular objects and two with plural objects.
