# Chapter 15 Seven-Day Review Plan

## Day 1

Study **Future tense**. Say examples, then write them from memory.

## Day 2

Recall Day 1 without notes. Study **Conditional** and complete the first three transformations.

## Day 3

Study **Earlier past and unreal past**. Create four original examples and read them aloud.

## Day 4

Study **Tout and indefinites**. Complete the remaining transformations.

## Day 5

Complete controlled production and four-way variation. Review only recorded mistakes.

## Day 6

Do dictation and the chapter production task. Read your writing aloud twice.

## Day 7

Redo Section A closed-book, explain all four rules aloud, and produce six new sentences.

## Spaced reviews

Repeat a ten-minute closed-book check on Days 10, 14, and 30:

1. write the key forms or rule from memory;
2. transform three sentences;
3. write two original sentences;
4. review only errors.
