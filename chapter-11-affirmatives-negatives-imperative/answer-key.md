## Sections C-F

These are productive tasks. Check them using this rule summary:

- **Expanded negatives:** Pairs include toujours/ne...jamais, déjà/ne...pas encore, quelqu'un/ne...personne, quelque chose/ne...rien, and encore/ne...plus.
- **Stressed pronouns:** Forms are moi, toi, lui, elle, nous, vous, eux, elles. Use them after prepositions, for emphasis, comparisons, and short answers.
- **Imperative forms:** Commands use tu, nous, and vous forms without subject pronouns. Regular -er tu commands normally drop final -s.
- **Commands with pronouns:** In affirmative commands, pronouns follow the verb with hyphens and their order changes. In negative commands, they return before the verb.

## Self-scoring

Award one point for each Section A answer and two points for each structurally correct Section B sentence.

- **85-100%:** proceed and schedule delayed review.
- **70-84%:** review only missed rules and retry the next day.
- **Below 70%:** repeat the lesson in two smaller sessions.

A corrected answer is not mastered until you can produce a different correct example on a later day.
