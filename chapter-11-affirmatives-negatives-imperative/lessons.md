# Chapter 11 Lessons: Affirmatives, Negatives, Stressed Pronouns, and the Imperative

Use short sessions. Accuracy from memory matters more than copying volume.

## Unit 1: Expanded negatives

Pairs include toujours/ne...jamais, déjà/ne...pas encore, quelqu'un/ne...personne, quelque chose/ne...rien, and encore/ne...plus.

### Examples

- `Je ne vois personne.` — I do not see anyone.
- `Elle ne mange jamais ici.` — She never eats here.
- `Nous n'avons rien.` — We have nothing.

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 2: Stressed pronouns

Forms are moi, toi, lui, elle, nous, vous, eux, elles. Use them after prepositions, for emphasis, comparisons, and short answers.

### Examples

- `avec moi` — with me
- `Lui, il comprend.` — He understands.
- `Elle travaille plus que nous.` — She works more than we do.

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 3: Imperative forms

Commands use tu, nous, and vous forms without subject pronouns. Regular -er tu commands normally drop final -s.

### Examples

- `Parle !` — Speak!
- `Finissons !` — Let's finish!
- `Attendez !` — Wait!
- `Soyez prudents !` — Be careful!

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 4: Commands with pronouns

In affirmative commands, pronouns follow the verb with hyphens and their order changes. In negative commands, they return before the verb.

### Examples

- `Regarde-le !` — Look at it/him!
- `Donnez-lui le livre !` — Give him/her the book!
- `Ne le regardez pas !` — Do not look at it/him!

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Chapter production task

Write house or study instructions containing four affirmative commands, four negative commands, and three stressed pronouns.

After writing, circle conjugated verbs, underline agreement or pronoun choices, and read the passage aloud twice.

## Sentence usage practice

Use these complete sentences to practice the chapter structures in context. Read each sentence aloud twice, cover it, and write it from memory.

1. `Moi, je ne vois personne dans le jardin.` — As for me, I do not see anyone in the garden.
2. `Ne laissez rien sur la table !` — Do not leave anything on the table!
3. `Donne-moi le marteau et apporte-le-lui ensuite.` — Give me the hammer and then bring it to him.
4. `Eux, ils ne travaillent jamais le dimanche.` — As for them, they never work on Sundays.

### Notice and mark

For each sentence, circle the target verb or grammar structure. Underline the word or phrase that controls its form.

### Change the sentence

Rewrite every sentence twice:

1. change the subject, person, or number;
2. change one meaningful detail such as the place, object, time, or description.

Make every required change to conjugation, agreement, articles, pronouns, and word order.

### Make it personal

Write four new sentences about your own life using the same four chapter structures. Read them aloud, check them, and rewrite only the parts you corrected.
