## Sections C-F

These are productive tasks. Check them using this rule summary:

- **Possessives:** Possessive adjectives agree with the possessed noun, not the owner. Use mon before a feminine noun beginning with a vowel sound.
- **Demonstratives:** Use ce, cet, cette, and ces before nouns. Demonstrative pronouns replace nouns: celui, celle, ceux, celles.
- **Comparisons:** Use plus...que, moins...que, and aussi...que. Compare nouns with plus de/moins de/autant de, and verbs with plus/moins/autant.
- **Adverbs:** Many adverbs derive from the feminine adjective plus -ment. Their placement depends on the tense and length.

## Self-scoring

Award one point for each Section A answer and two points for each structurally correct Section B sentence.

- **85-100%:** proceed and schedule delayed review.
- **70-84%:** review only missed rules and retry the next day.
- **Below 70%:** repeat the lesson in two smaller sessions.

A corrected answer is not mastered until you can produce a different correct example on a later day.
