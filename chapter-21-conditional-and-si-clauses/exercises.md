# Chapter 21 Exercises: Conditional Forms and Si Clauses

Work without notes; explain the form as well as the meaning. Reveal each answer block only after attempting its section.

## A. Conditional formation

1. je + être, present conditional
2. nous + faire, present conditional
3. vous + pouvoir, present conditional
4. elle + venir, past conditional
5. ils + écrire, past conditional
6. elles + se lever, past conditional

:::answer
1. `je serais` — ser- plus -ais.
2. `nous ferions` — fer- plus -ions.
3. `vous pourriez` — pourr- plus -iez.
4. `elle serait venue` — conditional être and feminine agreement.
5. `ils auraient écrit` — conditional avoir, irregular participle.
6. `elles se seraient levées` — reflexive direct-object agreement.
:::

## B. Match each condition

Translate and explain.
1. If you come tomorrow, we will cook together.
2. If you came tomorrow, we would cook together.
3. If you had come yesterday, we would have cooked together. (male addressee)
4. When we were children, we would play here.

:::answer
1. `Si tu viens demain, nous cuisinerons ensemble.` — present + future, open possibility.
2. `Si tu venais demain, nous cuisinerions ensemble.` — imparfait + conditional, imagined possibility.
3. `Si tu étais venu hier, nous aurions cuisiné ensemble.` — plus-que-parfait + past conditional, unreal past.
4. `Quand nous étions enfants, nous jouions ici.` — real repeated past, not conditional.
:::

## C. A missed opportunity

Complete: Si nous (savoir) l'heure hier, nous (partir) plus tôt. Maintenant, si nous (prendre: present) un taxi, nous (arriver: future) à temps. À ta place, je (appeler: conditional) le chauffeur. Use a mixed group.

:::answer
`Si nous avions su l'heure hier, nous serions partis plus tôt. Maintenant, si nous prenons un taxi, nous arriverons à temps. À ta place, j'appellerais le chauffeur.` — unreal past, then a still-open present condition, then advice. No conditional belongs in either si clause.
:::

## D. Independent advice and regret

Write four sentences: a polite request, advice, an imagined present condition, and a regret with an unreal past condition. Include both auxiliaries across the conditional forms.

:::answer
Model: `Pourriez-vous fermer la fenêtre ? Tu devrais prendre un manteau. Si j'avais une voiture, je t'accompagnerais. Si j'avais reçu ton message, je serais venue.` — female narrator; polite pouvoir, advisory devoir, imparfait + present conditional, plus-que-parfait + past conditional. Venue agrees with the narrator.
:::
