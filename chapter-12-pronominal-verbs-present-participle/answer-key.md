## Sections C-F

These are productive tasks. Check them using this rule summary:

- **Reflexive verbs:** Use me, te, se, nous, vous, se before the conjugated verb when the subject acts on itself.
- **Questions, negatives, commands:** Keep the reflexive pronoun before the verb in negatives and most questions. In affirmative commands, use toi/nous/vous after the verb.
- **Body parts and reciprocal meaning:** French often uses the definite article for body parts when possession is clear. Plural subjects may produce reciprocal meaning.
- **Present participle:** Usually form it from the nous stem minus -ons plus -ant. It can express simultaneous action, often with en.

## Self-scoring

Award one point for each Section A answer and two points for each structurally correct Section B sentence.

- **85-100%:** proceed and schedule delayed review.
- **70-84%:** review only missed rules and retry the next day.
- **Below 70%:** repeat the lesson in two smaller sessions.

A corrected answer is not mastered until you can produce a different correct example on a later day.
