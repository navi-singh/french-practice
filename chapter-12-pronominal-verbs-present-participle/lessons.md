# Chapter 12 Lessons: Pronominal Verbs and the Present Participle

Use short sessions. Accuracy from memory matters more than copying volume.

## Unit 1: Reflexive verbs

Use me, te, se, nous, vous, se before the conjugated verb when the subject acts on itself.

### Examples

- `Je me lève.` — I get up.
- `Nous nous préparons.` — We get ready.
- `Elles se reposent.` — They rest.

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 2: Questions, negatives, commands

Keep the reflexive pronoun before the verb in negatives and most questions. In affirmative commands, use toi/nous/vous after the verb.

### Examples

- `Elle ne se couche pas.` — She does not go to bed.
- `Vous vous levez ?` — Are you getting up?
- `Lève-toi !` — Get up!

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 3: Body parts and reciprocal meaning

French often uses the definite article for body parts when possession is clear. Plural subjects may produce reciprocal meaning.

### Examples

- `Je me lave les mains.` — I wash my hands.
- `Ils se parlent.` — They speak to each other.
- `Nous nous écrivons.` — We write to each other.

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 4: Present participle

Usually form it from the nous stem minus -ons plus -ant. It can express simultaneous action, often with en.

### Examples

- `parlant` — speaking
- `finissant` — finishing
- `en travaillant` — while/by working
- `En marchant, elle téléphone.` — While walking, she makes a phone call.

### Active writing

2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Chapter production task

Write your morning routine in eight sentences and add two simultaneous actions with en + present participle.

After writing, circle conjugated verbs, underline agreement or pronoun choices, and read the passage aloud twice.

## Sentence usage practice

Use these complete sentences to practice the chapter structures in context. Read each sentence aloud twice, cover it, and write it from memory.

1. `Je me lève tôt et je me prépare rapidement.` — I get up early and get ready quickly.
2. `Les enfants se lavent les mains avant le dîner.` — The children wash their hands before dinner.
3. `Nous nous parlons en marchant vers le travail.` — We talk to each other while walking to work.
4. `En étudiant chaque jour, elle fait des progrès.` — By studying every day, she makes progress.

### Notice and mark

For each sentence, circle the target verb or grammar structure. Underline the word or phrase that controls its form.

### Change the sentence

Rewrite every sentence twice:

1. change the subject, person, or number;
2. change one meaningful detail such as the place, object, time, or description.

Make every required change to conjugation, agreement, articles, pronouns, and word order.

### Make it personal

Write four new sentences about your own life using the same four chapter structures. Read them aloud, check them, and rewrite only the parts you corrected.
