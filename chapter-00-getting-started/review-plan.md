# Chapter 0 Seven-Day Review Plan

Pronunciation improves through **short daily contact**, not long single sessions. Ten focused minutes a day beats one two-hour session every week.

## Day 1

Study **Unit 1 (alphabet and accents)** and **Unit 2 (silent letters)**. Spell your own name aloud in French. Say the pairs `petit`/`petite` and `grand`/`grande` ten times each.

## Day 2

Recall Day 1 without notes: name the five accents and the CaReFuL letters. Then study **Unit 3 (vowel sounds)**. Spend five full minutes on the `u` versus `ou` minimal pairs.

## Day 3

Study **Unit 4 (nasal vowels)**. Say `bon`/`bonne`, `un`/`une`, and `canadien`/`canadienne` aloud five times each. Complete Sections A-C of `exercises.md`.

## Day 4

Study **Unit 5 (consonants)** and **Unit 6 (elision, liaison, rhythm)**. Complete Sections D-F. Read the "mon premier jour" passage aloud once, slowly.

## Day 5

Study **Unit 7 (greetings and `tu`/`vous`)**. Complete Sections G-H and J. Memorize the two conversations in `survival-phrases.md`, section 12.

## Day 6

Study **Unit 8 (numbers and introductions)**. Complete Section I. Write and memorize your five-line self-introduction, then record it.

## Day 7

Closed-book review:

1. redo Sections A-J with no notes and score yourself;
2. record the full final production assignment from `lessons.md`;
3. say your self-introduction and the bakery script from memory;
4. review only the items you missed.

## Spaced reviews

Repeat a ten-minute closed-book check on Days 10, 14, and 30:

1. read five unfamiliar French sentences aloud from a later chapter;
2. say three `u`/`ou` minimal pairs;
3. say three masculine/feminine pairs with a nasal contrast;
4. give your self-introduction from memory;
5. review only errors.

## Daily background habit

While you work through Chapters 1-16, keep one two-minute habit:

> Read any three French example sentences from the current chapter **aloud**, then tap to hear them and compare.

This is the single highest-value routine in the whole course. Pronunciation decays without use, and every later chapter gives you fresh material to practice on.

## Readiness test for Chapter 1

You are ready to move on when you can do all five of these:

| Check | Standard |
|---|---|
| Cold reading | Read a French sentence you have never seen aloud without freezing. |
| Minimal pair | Say `tu` and `tout` so a listener could tell them apart. |
| Gender by ear | Hear the difference between `bon` and `bonne`. |
| Elision | Correctly join `je` + `ai` and `le` + `école` without thinking. |
| Social French | Greet, introduce yourself, and order something from memory. |

If you fail one, repeat only the matching unit. Do not repeat the whole chapter.

## What to carry into Chapter 1

Keep these three things active from now on:

1. **The habit of reading aloud.** Never read a French example silently.
2. **`glossary.md`.** Look up every grammar term the moment you meet it.
3. **The politeness reflex.** `Bonjour` first, `s'il vous plaît` with every request, `vous` when in doubt.
