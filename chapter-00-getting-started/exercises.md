# Chapter 0 Exercises

Complete these with the lessons closed. Put a question mark beside any answer you guessed, and check those first in the answer key.

Sections A-J have definite answers. Sections K-L are productive tasks.

## A. Silent letters

Is the **final consonant** pronounced? Answer yes or no, then say the word aloud.

1. `petit`
2. `avec`
3. `bonjour`
4. `vous`
5. `neuf`
6. `parler`
7. `avril`
8. `grand`

## B. `u` versus `ou`

In each pair, which word contains the "oo" sound?

1. `tu` / `tout`
2. `rue` / `roue`
3. `vu` / `vous`
4. `sur` / `sourd`

Say both words in each pair aloud before answering.

## C. Nasal vowels

Does the word contain a nasal vowel? Answer nasal or not nasal, and give the reason.

1. `bon`
2. `bonne`
3. `américain`
4. `américaine`
5. `téléphone`
6. `combien`
7. `un`
8. `une`

## D. Elision

Combine the words correctly.

1. `je` + `ai`
2. `le` + `hôtel`
3. `ne` + `est` + `pas`
4. `de` + `eau`
5. `que` + `elle`
6. `la` + `école`

## E. Liaison

Mark where two words link, and give the **linking sound**. If there is no liaison, say so and explain why.

1. `les amis`
2. `vous avez`
3. `un grand arbre`
4. `mon ami`
5. `un livre et un stylo`
6. `nous allons`

## F. Consonant sounds

Give the sound of the letter or letters in bold type.

1. `ça` — the **ç**
2. `le garçon` — the **ç**
3. `la chaise` — the **ch**
4. `qui` — the **qu**
5. `la montagne` — the **gn**
6. `la maison` — the **s**
7. `le poisson` — the **ss**
8. `je` — the **j**

## G. `tu` or `vous`?

Choose the correct form of address.

1. Your closest friend.
2. A waiter you have just met.
3. Two children together.
4. Your grandmother.
5. A new manager at your workplace.
6. Your younger brother.

## H. What would you say?

Give one appropriate French sentence for each situation.

1. You walk into a bakery at ten in the morning.
2. You want to order a coffee politely.
3. Someone has just thanked you.
4. You did not understand what a person said.
5. You want the person to speak more slowly.
6. You are leaving a shop at midday.

## J. Find and fix the error

Each sentence contains one mistake a beginner commonly makes. Rewrite it correctly and name the rule.

1. `Je habite à Paris.`
2. `Je suis vingt ans.`
3. You arrive at a friend's home at 8 p.m. and say: `Bonne nuit !`
4. In a café you say: `Je veux un café.`
5. You ask a stranger on the street: `Tu parles anglais ?`

## K. Production

1. Write a five-line self-introduction: name, age, nationality, city, and language level. Memorize it and say it aloud without reading.
2. Write the exact four lines you would say in a bakery: greeting, request, thanks, farewell.
3. Choose five phrases from `survival-phrases.md` that you personally will need this week. Write them out and use each one aloud today.

## L. Dictation and oral recall

1. **Dictation.** Use text-to-speech on the website to hear six sentences from the "Sentence usage practice" sections of `lessons.md`. Write what you hear without looking. Correct in two passes: first the sounds you missed, then the silent letters and accents you left out.
2. **Read aloud.** Read the "Connected reading: mon premier jour" passage aloud twice. Record the second attempt and check it for even syllable rhythm.
3. **Oral recall.** Explain aloud, in simple English, and then give one French example from memory:
   - which final consonants are usually pronounced;
   - what makes a vowel nasal;
   - the difference between elision and liaison;
   - when to use `vous` instead of `tu`.
