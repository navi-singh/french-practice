# Chapter 20 Exercises: The Future Family

Work without notes; explain the form as well as the meaning. Reveal each answer block only after attempting its section.

## A. Build three futures

1. nous + partir, futur proche
2. je + être, futur simple
3. vous + envoyer, futur simple
4. elle + finir, futur antérieur
5. elles + arriver, futur antérieur
6. tu + se coucher, futur proche

:::answer
1. `nous allons partir` — conjugate aller, not partir.
2. `je serai` — ser- plus -ai.
3. `vous enverrez` — enverr- plus -ez.
4. `elle aura fini` — future avoir plus participle.
5. `elles seront arrivées` — future être and feminine plural agreement.
6. `tu vas te coucher` — reflexive pronoun stays with infinitive.
:::

## B. Choose a future viewpoint

Translate and explain.
1. Look! That glass is going to fall.
2. I promise I will call you.
3. By ten, we will have finished the meeting.
4. If you are free, we will visit the museum.

:::answer
1. `Regarde ! Ce verre va tomber.` — prediction grounded in visible evidence.
2. `Je te promets que je t'appellerai.` — futur simple for the promise.
3. `À dix heures, nous aurons fini la réunion.` — completion by a future point.
4. `Si tu es libre, nous visiterons le musée.` — present after conditional si, future in the result.
:::

## C. Future sequence

Complete: Demain, nous (prendre: simple future) le train. À huit heures, Léa (préparer: future perfect) les sandwiches. Quand nous (arriver: future perfect), nous (appeler: simple future) nos amis. Use a mixed group.

:::answer
`Demain, nous prendrons le train. À huit heures, Léa aura préparé les sandwiches. Quand nous serons arrivés, nous appellerons nos amis.` — future actions: prendrons/appellerons; preparations completed by eight and arrival completed before the call: futur antérieur.
:::

## D. Independent future plan

Write a short plan containing a present intention, a prediction, a completed-by deadline, and a future time clause. Do not put futur simple after conditional si.

:::answer
Model: `Je vais préparer mon sac ce soir. Il fera froid demain. À sept heures, j'aurai terminé mes préparatifs. Quand le taxi arrivera, je descendrai.` — current intention, forecast, completion deadline, then a future time clause. A real-condition alternative is `Si le taxi arrive tôt, je serai prêt.`
:::
