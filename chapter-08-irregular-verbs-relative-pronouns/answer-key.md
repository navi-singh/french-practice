## Sections C-F

These are productive tasks. Check them using this rule summary:

- **Savoir versus connaître:** Savoir is used for facts, information, and knowing how; connaître is used for people, places, and familiarity.
- **Irregular verb families:** Learn voir/croire, courir/rire, offrir, conduire, suivre/vivre, and craindre through frequent first-person and plural forms.
- **Qui, que, and où:** Qui is the subject of the relative clause; que is its direct object; où refers to a place or time.
- **Dont and lequel:** Dont replaces de + noun. Forms of lequel commonly follow prepositions and agree with their antecedent.

## Self-scoring

Award one point for each Section A answer and two points for each structurally correct Section B sentence.

- **85-100%:** proceed and schedule delayed review.
- **70-84%:** review only missed rules and retry the next day.
- **Below 70%:** repeat the lesson in two smaller sessions.

A corrected answer is not mastered until you can produce a different correct example on a later day.
