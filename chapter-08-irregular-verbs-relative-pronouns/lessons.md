# Chapter 8 Lessons: Irregular Verbs II and Relative Pronouns

Use short sessions. Accuracy from memory matters more than copying volume.

## Unit 1: Savoir versus connaître

Savoir is used for facts, information, and knowing how; connaître is used for people, places, and familiarity.

### Examples

- `Je sais la réponse.` — I know the answer.
- `Elle sait conduire.` — She knows how to drive.
- `Nous connaissons Paris.` — We know Paris.

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 3: Qui, que, and où

Qui is the subject of the relative clause; que is its direct object; où refers to a place or time.

### Examples

- `La femme qui parle...` — The woman who is speaking...
- `Le livre que je lis...` — The book that I am reading...
- `La ville où j'habite...` — The city where I live...

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 4: Dont and lequel

Dont replaces de + noun. Forms of lequel commonly follow prepositions and agree with their antecedent.

### Examples

- `L'ami dont je parle...` — The friend whom I am talking about...
- `La table sur laquelle...` — The table on which...
- `Le projet auquel je pense...` — The project that I am thinking about...

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Chapter production task

Describe a profession or person in six sentences. Include savoir, connaître, and four different relative pronouns.

After writing, circle conjugated verbs, underline agreement or pronoun choices, and read the passage aloud twice.

## Sentence usage practice

Use these complete sentences to practice the chapter structures in context. Read each sentence aloud twice, cover it, and write it from memory.

1. `Je connais la femme qui dirige cette entreprise.` — I know the woman who runs this company.
2. `Voici le livre que mon professeur recommande.` — Here is the book that my teacher recommends.
3. `Montréal est la ville où ils veulent vivre.` — Montreal is the city where they want to live.
4. `C'est un métier dont elle parle souvent.` — It is a profession that she often talks about.

### Notice and mark

For each sentence, circle the target verb or grammar structure. Underline the word or phrase that controls its form.

### Change the sentence

Rewrite every sentence twice:

1. change the subject, person, or number;
2. change one meaningful detail such as the place, object, time, or description.

Make every required change to conjugation, agreement, articles, pronouns, and word order.

### Make it personal

Write four new sentences about your own life using the same four chapter structures. Read them aloud, check them, and rewrite only the parts you corrected.
