# Chapter 4 Exercises

Complete these without notes. Put a question mark beside uncertain answers.

## A. Transform or translate

1. 42
2. 1 May
3. 14:30
4. nous / finir
5. elles / choisir
6. vous / réussir

## B. Controlled production

1. Write a new sentence demonstrating **numbers and dates**. Use `le premier mai` only as a structural model; change its content.
2. Write a new sentence demonstrating **time**. Use `Il est huit heures.` only as a structural model; change its content.
3. Write a new sentence demonstrating **regular -ir verbs**. Use `Je finis tôt.` only as a structural model; change its content.
4. Write a new sentence demonstrating **functional practice**. Use `Le magasin ouvre à neuf heures.` only as a structural model; change its content.

## C. Four-way variation

Choose one sentence from each lesson unit and rewrite it four ways:

1. change the subject;
2. make it negative;
3. turn it into a question;
4. change one meaningful detail.

## D. Dictation and correction

Use text-to-speech to hear six examples from `lessons.md`. Write what you hear without looking. Correct in separate passes:

1. vocabulary;
2. verb form;
3. agreement or pronoun placement;
4. spelling and accents.

## E. Personal writing

Write a one-day schedule with five times, three prices, and four regular -ir verb forms.

## F. Oral recall

Explain each of the chapter's four main rules aloud in simple English, then give one French example from memory.
