## Sections C-F

These are productive tasks. Check them using this rule summary:

- **Numbers and dates:** Practice numbers in meaningful chunks: prices, addresses, years, and phone numbers. Dates use cardinal numbers except premier for the first.
- **Time:** Use Il est... for clock time. Add du matin, de l'après-midi, or du soir when useful. Midi and minuit are special forms.
- **Regular -ir verbs:** For verbs like finir, remove -ir and add -is, -is, -it, -issons, -issez, -issent.
- **Functional practice:** Combine quantities, time, and -ir verbs in realistic situations such as stores, meals, and appointments.

## Self-scoring

Award one point for each Section A answer and two points for each structurally correct Section B sentence.

- **85-100%:** proceed and schedule delayed review.
- **70-84%:** review only missed rules and retry the next day.
- **Below 70%:** repeat the lesson in two smaller sessions.

A corrected answer is not mastered until you can produce a different correct example on a later day.
