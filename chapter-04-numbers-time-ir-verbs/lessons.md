# Chapter 4 Lessons: Numbers, Dates, Time, and Regular -ir Verbs

Use short sessions. Accuracy from memory matters more than copying volume.

## Unit 1: Numbers and dates

Practice numbers in meaningful chunks: prices, addresses, years, and phone numbers. Dates use cardinal numbers except premier for the first.

### Examples

- `le premier mai` — May first
- `le quinze septembre` — September fifteenth
- `deux mille vingt-six` — two thousand twenty-six

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 2: Time

Use Il est... for clock time. Add du matin, de l'après-midi, or du soir when useful. Midi and minuit are special forms.

### Examples

- `Il est huit heures.` — It is eight o'clock.
- `Il est midi et quart.` — It is a quarter past noon.
- `Il est dix-neuf heures trente.` — It is 7:30 p.m.

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 3: Regular -ir verbs

For verbs like finir, remove -ir and add -is, -is, -it, -issons, -issez, -issent.

### Examples

- `Je finis tôt.` — I finish early.
- `Nous choisissons le menu.` — We choose the menu.
- `Elles réussissent.` — They succeed.

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 4: Functional practice

Combine quantities, time, and -ir verbs in realistic situations such as stores, meals, and appointments.

### Examples

- `Le magasin ouvre à neuf heures.` — The store opens at nine o'clock.
- `Nous choisissons deux desserts.` — We choose two desserts.
- `Vous finissez à quelle heure ?` — What time do you finish?

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Chapter production task

Write a one-day schedule with five times, three prices, and four regular -ir verb forms.

After writing, circle conjugated verbs, underline agreement or pronoun choices, and read the passage aloud twice.

## Sentence usage practice

Use these complete sentences to practice the chapter structures in context. Read each sentence aloud twice, cover it, and write it from memory.

1. `Le cours commence à neuf heures quinze.` — The class starts at 9:15.
2. `Mon anniversaire est le premier novembre.` — My birthday is November first.
3. `Nous choisissons deux desserts pour huit euros.` — We choose two desserts for eight euros.
4. `Les magasins finissent leur journée à dix-neuf heures.` — The stores finish their day at 7 p.m.

### Notice and mark

For each sentence, circle the target verb or grammar structure. Underline the word or phrase that controls its form.

### Change the sentence

Rewrite every sentence twice:

1. change the subject, person, or number;
2. change one meaningful detail such as the place, object, time, or description.

Make every required change to conjugation, agreement, articles, pronouns, and word order.

### Make it personal

Write four new sentences about your own life using the same four chapter structures. Read them aloud, check them, and rewrite only the parts you corrected.
