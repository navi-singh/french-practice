# Chapter 4: Numbers, Dates, Time, and Regular -ir Verbs

This is an original companion learning pack based on the chapter's grammar sequence. It does not reproduce the textbook.

## Outcomes

- Say cardinal and ordinal numbers.
- Write dates and tell time.
- Conjugate regular -ir verbs.
- Use shopping and meal vocabulary in short exchanges.

## Lesson sequence

1. Numbers and dates
2. Time
3. Regular -ir verbs
4. Functional practice

## Recommended workflow

1. Read one unit in `lessons.md`.
2. Say every French example aloud twice.
3. Cover the examples and rewrite them from memory.
4. Complete the matching transformation in `exercises.md`.
5. Check `answer-key.md` only after finishing.
6. Record the rule behind each error.
7. Follow the spaced plan in `review-plan.md`.

## Mastery standard

Continue when you can score at least 85%, produce six original sentences, and explain your corrections without notes.
