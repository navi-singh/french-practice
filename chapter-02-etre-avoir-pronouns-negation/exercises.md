# Chapter 2 Exercises

Complete these without notes. Put a question mark beside uncertain answers.

## A. Transform or translate

1. Je suis content.
2. Nous avons une maison.
3. Il y a des fenêtres.
4. Marie et Léa sont françaises.
5. Paul a faim.
6. Tu es en retard.

## B. Controlled production

1. Write a new sentence demonstrating **subject pronouns**. Use `Je suis ici.` only as a structural model; change its content.
2. Write a new sentence demonstrating **être**. Use `Nous sommes calmes.` only as a structural model; change its content.
3. Write a new sentence demonstrating **avoir and il y a**. Use `J'ai vingt ans.` only as a structural model; change its content.
4. Write a new sentence demonstrating **negation**. Use `Je ne suis pas fatigué.` only as a structural model; change its content.

## C. Four-way variation

Choose one sentence from each lesson unit and rewrite it four ways:

1. change the subject;
2. make it negative;
3. turn it into a question;
4. change one meaningful detail.

## D. Dictation and correction

Use text-to-speech to hear six examples from `lessons.md`. Write what you hear without looking. Correct in separate passes:

1. vocabulary;
2. verb form;
3. agreement or pronoun placement;
4. spelling and accents.

## E. Personal writing

Write six sentences introducing yourself and your home. Use être twice, avoir twice, il y a once, and one negative.

## F. Oral recall

Explain each of the chapter's four main rules aloud in simple English, then give one French example from memory.
