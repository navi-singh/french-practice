## Sections C-F

These are productive tasks. Check them using this rule summary:

- **Subject pronouns:** French verbs change with je, tu, il/elle/on, nous, vous, and ils/elles. Use vous for a plural group or polite singular address.
- **Être:** Present forms: suis, es, est, sommes, êtes, sont. Use être for identity, description, nationality, location in many basic statements, and selected expressions.
- **Avoir and il y a:** Present forms: ai, as, a, avons, avez, ont. French uses avoir in many expressions where English uses “to be.” Il y a means there is or there are.
- **Negation:** Place ne before the conjugated verb and pas after it. Before a vowel sound, ne becomes n'. With indefinite or partitive objects after a negative avoir, the article often becomes de.

## Self-scoring

Award one point for each Section A answer and two points for each structurally correct Section B sentence.

- **85-100%:** proceed and schedule delayed review.
