# Chapter 9 Lessons: Prendre, Boire, Partitive Articles, and Object Pronouns

Use short sessions. Accuracy from memory matters more than copying volume.

## Unit 1: Prendre and boire

Practice these verbs in meals, transportation, and daily choices.

### Examples

- `Je prends le métro.` — I take the subway.
- `Nous prenons un café.` — We have a coffee.
- `Elle boit de l'eau.` — She drinks water.

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 2: Partitive articles

Use du, de la, de l', and des for an unspecified quantity. After many negatives and quantity expressions, use de.

### Examples

- `du pain` — some bread
- `de la soupe` — some soup
- `de l'eau` — some water
- `Je ne bois pas de lait.` — I do not drink milk.

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 3: Direct and indirect objects

Place object pronouns before the conjugated verb: le, la, les for direct objects; lui and leur for many à + person objects.

### Examples

- `Je le vois.` — I see him/it.
- `Nous lui parlons.` — We speak to him/her.
- `Elle les invite.` — She invites them.

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 4: Y and en

Y often replaces à + thing or a place. En often replaces de + thing or a quantity phrase.

### Examples

- `J'y vais.` — I am going there.
- `Elle en parle.` — She talks about it.
- `Nous en achetons deux.` — We buy two of them.

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Chapter production task

Write a grocery or café dialogue using two partitives, two direct objects, one indirect object, y, and en.

After writing, circle conjugated verbs, underline agreement or pronoun choices, and read the passage aloud twice.

## Sentence usage practice

Use these complete sentences to practice the chapter structures in context. Read each sentence aloud twice, cover it, and write it from memory.

1. `Je prends du pain et je le mets sur la table.` — I take some bread and put it on the table.
2. `Elle parle à ses voisins et elle leur offre du café.` — She speaks to her neighbors and offers them coffee.
3. `Nous allons au marché le samedi et nous y achetons des légumes.` — We go to the market on Saturdays and buy vegetables there.
4. `Il veut trois pommes, alors il en prend trois.` — He wants three apples, so he takes three of them.

### Notice and mark

For each sentence, circle the target verb or grammar structure. Underline the word or phrase that controls its form.

### Change the sentence

Rewrite every sentence twice:

1. change the subject, person, or number;
2. change one meaningful detail such as the place, object, time, or description.

Make every required change to conjugation, agreement, articles, pronouns, and word order.

### Make it personal

Write four new sentences about your own life using the same four chapter structures. Read them aloud, check them, and rewrite only the parts you corrected.
