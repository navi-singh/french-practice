# Chapter 7 Lessons: Irregular Verbs I and Verb + Infinitive Constructions

Use short sessions. Accuracy from memory matters more than copying volume.

## Unit 1: Movement and arrival

Partir means to leave/depart, sortir means to go out or take out, quitter takes a direct object, and venir means to come.

### Examples

- `Je pars demain.` — I am leaving tomorrow.
- `Elle sort ce soir.` — She is going out tonight.
- `Nous quittons la ville.` — We are leaving the city.

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 2: Communication and action

## Unit 3: Ability, desire, obligation

Pouvoir, vouloir, and devoir are followed directly by an infinitive.

### Examples

- `Je peux venir.` — I can come.
- `Nous voulons apprendre.` — We want to learn.
- `Tu dois travailler.` — You must work.

## Unit 4: Verb + verb patterns

Some verbs take no preposition, some take à, and others take de before an infinitive. Memorize the whole frame.

### Examples

- `Elle aime lire.` — She likes to read.
- `Il commence à écrire.` — He is beginning to write.
- `Nous essayons de comprendre.` — We are trying to understand.

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Chapter production task

Write eight decisions for the coming month using pouvoir, vouloir, devoir, commencer à, and essayer de.

After writing, circle conjugated verbs, underline agreement or pronoun choices, and read the passage aloud twice.

## Sentence usage practice

Use these complete sentences to practice the chapter structures in context. Read each sentence aloud twice, cover it, and write it from memory.

1. `Je veux lire davantage, mais je dois finir mon travail.` — I want to read more, but I must finish my work.
2. `Nous pouvons sortir après avoir écrit le message.` — We can go out after writing the message.
3. `Elle commence à apprendre le français et essaie de parler chaque jour.` — She is beginning to learn French and tries to speak every day.
4. `Ils quittent le bureau et partent à six heures.` — They leave the office and depart at six o'clock.

### Notice and mark

For each sentence, circle the target verb or grammar structure. Underline the word or phrase that controls its form.

### Change the sentence

Rewrite every sentence twice:

1. change the subject, person, or number;
2. change one meaningful detail such as the place, object, time, or description.

Make every required change to conjugation, agreement, articles, pronouns, and word order.

### Make it personal

Write four new sentences about your own life using the same four chapter structures. Read them aloud, check them, and rewrite only the parts you corrected.
