3. `Elle commence à écrire.`

5. `Je quitte la maison.`
6. `Nous sortons ce soir.`

## Section B

Answers vary. A correct sentence must clearly demonstrate the named structure, contain a properly conjugated verb where required, and preserve agreement and word order.

## Sections C-F

These are productive tasks. Check them using this rule summary:

- **Movement and arrival:** Partir means to leave/depart, sortir means to go out or take out, quitter takes a direct object, and venir means to come.
- **Communication and action:** Practice dire, lire, écrire, mettre, recevoir, and related forms as complete phrases.
- **Ability, desire, obligation:** Pouvoir, vouloir, and devoir are followed directly by an infinitive.
- **Verb + verb patterns:** Some verbs take no preposition, some take à, and others take de before an infinitive. Memorize the whole frame.

## Self-scoring

Award one point for each Section A answer and two points for each structurally correct Section B sentence.

- **85-100%:** proceed and schedule delayed review.
- **70-84%:** review only missed rules and retry the next day.
- **Below 70%:** repeat the lesson in two smaller sessions.

A corrected answer is not mastered until you can produce a different correct example on a later day.
