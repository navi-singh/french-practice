## Sections C-F

These are productive tasks. Check them using this rule summary:

- **Calendar language:** Days and months are normally lowercase. Use en with months and seasons in common date expressions, and le with a recurring day.
- **Regular -er verbs:** Remove -er and add -e, -es, -e, -ons, -ez, -ent. The written endings differ more than their pronunciation.
- **Yes/no questions:** Use rising intonation, est-ce que, or inversion. Est-ce que is reliable in everyday speech.
- **Information questions:** Combine question words such as où, quand, comment, pourquoi, combien, and qui with a question structure.

## Self-scoring

Award one point for each Section A answer and two points for each structurally correct Section B sentence.

- **85-100%:** proceed and schedule delayed review.
- **70-84%:** review only missed rules and retry the next day.
- **Below 70%:** repeat the lesson in two smaller sessions.

A corrected answer is not mastered until you can produce a different correct example on a later day.
