# Chapter 3 Lessons: Days, Months, Regular -er Verbs, and Questions

Use short sessions. Accuracy from memory matters more than copying volume.

## Unit 1: Calendar language

Days and months are normally lowercase. Use en with months and seasons in common date expressions, and le with a recurring day.

### Examples

- `Nous sommes lundi.` — It is Monday.
- `Je voyage en juillet.` — I travel in July.
- `Le samedi, je travaille.` — On Saturdays, I work.

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 2: Regular -er verbs

Remove -er and add -e, -es, -e, -ons, -ez, -ent. The written endings differ more than their pronunciation.

### Examples

- `Je parle français.` — I speak French.
- `Nous regardons un film.` — We are watching a movie.
- `Ils habitent ici.` — They live here.

1. Copy each example once while saying it.

## Unit 3: Yes/no questions

Use rising intonation, est-ce que, or inversion. Est-ce que is reliable in everyday speech.

### Examples

- `Tu travailles demain ?` — Are you working tomorrow?
- `Est-ce que vous aimez Paris ?` — Do you like Paris?
- `Parlez-vous anglais ?` — Do you speak English?

## Unit 4: Information questions

Combine question words such as où, quand, comment, pourquoi, combien, and qui with a question structure.

### Examples

- `Où habitez-vous ?` — Where do you live?
- `Quand est-ce qu'elle arrive ?` — When is she arriving?
- `Pourquoi étudient-ils ?` — Why are they studying?

4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.

## Chapter production task

Write a seven-line weekly routine. Include four -er verbs and two questions you could ask a classmate.

After writing, circle conjugated verbs, underline agreement or pronoun choices, and read the passage aloud twice.

## Sentence usage practice

Use these complete sentences to practice the chapter structures in context. Read each sentence aloud twice, cover it, and write it from memory.

1. `Le lundi, je travaille à la maison.` — On Mondays, I work at home.
2. `Nous étudions le français en septembre.` — We study French in September.
3. `Est-ce que vous regardez souvent des films français ?` — Do you often watch French movies?
4. `Pourquoi est-ce qu'elle arrive si tôt ?` — Why does she arrive so early?

### Notice and mark

For each sentence, circle the target verb or grammar structure. Underline the word or phrase that controls its form.

### Change the sentence

Rewrite every sentence twice:

1. change the subject, person, or number;
2. change one meaningful detail such as the place, object, time, or description.

Make every required change to conjugation, agreement, articles, pronouns, and word order.

### Make it personal

Write four new sentences about your own life using the same four chapter structures. Read them aloud, check them, and rewrite only the parts you corrected.
