# Chapter 16 Exercises

Complete these without notes. Put a question mark beside uncertain answers.

## A. Transform or translate

1. parler, que je
2. finir, que nous
3. être, qu'il
4. Il faut / vous venir
5. Je veux / tu réussir
6. I am happy that she is here.

:::answer
1. `que je parle` — Regular subjunctive from `ils parlent`: stem parl- plus -e.
2. `que nous finissions` — From `ils finissent`, stem finiss- plus -ions.
3. `qu'il soit` — **Être** is irregular in the subjunctive.
4. `Il faut que vous veniez.` — `il faut que` triggers the subjunctive; `venir` has `veniez` with `vous`.
5. `Je veux que tu réussisses.` — Desire plus a different subject triggers the subjunctive.
6. `Je suis content qu'elle soit ici.` — Emotion triggers the subjunctive; `être` becomes `soit`.
:::

## B. Controlled production

1. Write a new sentence demonstrating **forming the subjunctive**. Use `que je parle` only as a structural model; change its content.
2. Write a new sentence demonstrating **important irregulars**. Use `que je sois` only as a structural model; change its content.
3. Write a new sentence demonstrating **necessity and judgment**. Use `Il faut que vous veniez.` only as a structural model; change its content.
4. Write a new sentence demonstrating **volition, emotion, and doubt**. Use `Je veux que tu réussisses.` only as a structural model; change its content.

:::answer
Answers vary. A correct answer must show the named structure and change the model sentence's content.

1. **Forming the subjunctive:** `que tu vendes` — from `ils vendent`, stem vend-, ending -es.
2. **Important irregulars:** `que nous ayons le temps` — `avoir` is irregular: `ayons`.
3. **Necessity and judgment:** `Il est important que vous compreniez.` — judgment trigger plus subjunctive.
4. **Volition, emotion, and doubt:** `Je doute qu'ils puissent venir.` — doubt triggers the subjunctive; `pouvoir` becomes `puissent`.

Mark yourself wrong if you used the subjunctive after a certainty trigger such as `je sais que`, or if the same subject should have used an infinitive instead.
:::

## C. Four-way variation

Choose one sentence from each lesson unit and rewrite it four ways:

1. change the subject;
2. make it negative;
3. turn it into a question;
4. change one meaningful detail.

:::answer
Answers vary. Worked example from `Il faut que vous veniez avant neuf heures.`

1. Change the subject: `Il faut que tu viennes avant neuf heures.` — `venir` changes from `veniez` to `viennes`.
2. Make it negative: `Il ne faut pas que vous veniez avant neuf heures.` — the trigger becomes negative, but it still takes the subjunctive.
3. Turn it into a question: `Est-ce qu'il faut que vous veniez avant neuf heures ?` — the dependent verb remains subjunctive.
4. Change one meaningful detail: `Il faut que vous veniez après le déjeuner.` — the time changes; the trigger and mood stay the same.

For your own versions, identify the trigger before choosing the verb form.
:::

## D. Dictation and correction

Use text-to-speech to hear six examples from `lessons.md`. Write what you hear without looking. Correct in separate passes:

1. vocabulary;
2. verb form;
3. agreement or pronoun placement;
4. spelling and accents.

Do the dictation first, using this chapter's own examples as your audio. Then open the answers to check: they list six sentences worth dictating and the trap each one sets.

:::answer
Six sentences from this chapter that make good dictation material, and the trap each one sets:

1. `Il faut que vous arriviez avant neuf heures.` — The trap is `arriviez`, a subjunctive `vous` form that sounds close to other -iez forms.
2. `Je veux que tu réussisses.` — Desire plus a different subject triggers the subjunctive; the double `s` belongs to `réussisses`.
3. `Nous sommes contents qu'elle soit ici.` — Emotion triggers the subjunctive, and `être` becomes irregular `soit`.
4. `Il est possible qu'elle sache déjà la réponse.` — Possibility triggers the subjunctive; `savoir` becomes `sache`.
5. `Je ne pense pas qu'il vienne.` — Negative opinion triggers the subjunctive; `venir` becomes `vienne`.
6. `Je sais qu'il vient.` — Certainty uses the indicative, so this is `vient`, not `vienne`.

Correct by trigger first. If the trigger is wrong, the verb form will usually be wrong too.
:::

## E. Personal writing

Write eight recommendations for a friend using il faut que, je veux que, il est important que, and je suis content que.

:::answer
Answers vary. A model set of eight recommendations:

1. `Il faut que tu dormes plus tôt.`
2. `Il faut que tu boives plus d'eau.`
3. `Je veux que tu réussisses ton examen.`
4. `Je veux que tu prennes une vraie pause.`
5. `Il est important que tu sois honnête avec toi-même.`
6. `Il est important que tu fasses un planning simple.`
7. `Je suis content que tu demandes de l'aide.`
8. `Je suis content que tu veuilles progresser.`

Self-check: each sentence has a trigger plus `que`, a subject in the dependent clause, and a subjunctive verb. If the subject is the same as the main clause subject, an infinitive may be more natural.
:::

## F. Oral recall

Explain each of the chapter's four main rules aloud in simple English, then give one French example from memory.

:::answer
1. **Forming the subjunctive.** Many verbs start from the `ils` present stem, remove -ent, and add subjunctive endings. Example: `que je parle`.
2. **Important irregulars.** Forms such as `sois`, `aie`, `aille`, `fasse`, `puisse`, `sache`, and `veuille` must be memorized. Example: `Il faut qu'elle soit ici.`
3. **Necessity and judgment.** Triggers such as `il faut que`, `il est important que`, and `il vaut mieux que` usually take the subjunctive. Example: `Il faut que nous partions.`
4. **Volition, emotion, and doubt.** Desire, emotion, fear, doubt, and negative opinion often take the subjunctive when the subject changes. Example: `Je doute qu'il vienne.` Certainty triggers such as `je sais que` use the indicative instead.
:::
