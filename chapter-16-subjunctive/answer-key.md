## Sections C-F

These are productive tasks. Check them using this rule summary:

- **Forming the subjunctive:** For many verbs, start from the ils present stem, remove -ent, and add -e, -es, -e, -ions, -iez, -ent.
- **Important irregulars:** Master être, avoir, aller, faire, pouvoir, savoir, vouloir, and common stem-changing forms.
- **Necessity and judgment:** Impersonal expressions such as il faut que, il est important que, and il vaut mieux que commonly trigger the subjunctive.
- **Volition, emotion, and doubt:** A change of subject after vouloir, préférer, être content, avoir peur, douter, or a negative opinion often leads to que + subjunctive.

## Self-scoring
