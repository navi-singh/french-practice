# Chapter 16 Lessons: The Present Subjunctive

Use short sessions. Accuracy from memory matters more than copying volume.

## Unit 1: Forming the subjunctive

For many verbs, start from the ils present stem, remove -ent, and add -e, -es, -e, -ions, -iez, -ent.

### Examples

- `que je parle` — that I speak
- `que nous finissions` — that we finish
- `qu'ils attendent` — that they wait

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 2: Important irregulars

Master être, avoir, aller, faire, pouvoir, savoir, vouloir, and common stem-changing forms.

### Examples

- `que je sois` — that I be
- `que tu aies` — that you have
- `qu'elle aille` — that she go
- `que nous fassions` — that we do/make

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.

## Unit 3: Necessity and judgment

Impersonal expressions such as il faut que, il est important que, and il vaut mieux que commonly trigger the subjunctive.

### Examples

- `Il faut que vous veniez.` — You must come.
- `Il est important qu'elle comprenne.` — It is important that she understand.

4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.

## Unit 4: Volition, emotion, and doubt

A change of subject after vouloir, préférer, être content, avoir peur, douter, or a negative opinion often leads to que + subjunctive.

### Examples

- `Je veux que tu réussisses.` — I want you to succeed.
- `Nous sommes contents qu'il soit ici.` — We are happy that he is here.
- `Je ne pense pas qu'elle vienne.` — I do not think that she is coming.

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Chapter production task

Write eight recommendations for a friend using il faut que, je veux que, il est important que, and je suis content que.

After writing, circle conjugated verbs, underline agreement or pronoun choices, and read the passage aloud twice.

## Sentence usage practice

Use these complete sentences to practice the chapter structures in context. Read each sentence aloud twice, cover it, and write it from memory.

1. `Il faut que vous arriviez avant neuf heures.` — You must arrive before nine o'clock.
2. `Je veux que mes amis puissent venir à la fête.` — I want my friends to be able to come to the party.
3. `Nous sommes heureux qu'elle soit ici avec nous.` — We are happy that she is here with us.
4. `Je ne pense pas qu'il connaisse la réponse.` — I do not think that he knows the answer.

### Notice and mark

For each sentence, circle the target verb or grammar structure. Underline the word or phrase that controls its form.

### Change the sentence

Rewrite every sentence twice:

1. change the subject, person, or number;
2. change one meaningful detail such as the place, object, time, or description.

Make every required change to conjugation, agreement, articles, pronouns, and word order.

### Make it personal

Write four new sentences about your own life using the same four chapter structures. Read them aloud, check them, and rewrite only the parts you corrected.
