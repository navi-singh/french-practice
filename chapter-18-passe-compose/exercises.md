# Chapter 18 Exercises: Completed Events with the Passé Composé

Work without notes; explain the form as well as the meaning. Reveal each answer block only after attempting its section.

## A. Auxiliary and participle drill

1. nous + finir, completed past
2. elle + venir, completed past
3. ils + prendre, completed past
4. Marie + se lever, completed past
5. elle + sortir les chaises, completed past
6. elles + se parler, completed past

:::answer
1. `nous avons fini` — regular -ir participle, avoir.
2. `elle est venue` — intransitive venir takes être and feminine agreement.
3. `ils ont pris` — irregular participle, no subject agreement with avoir.
4. `Marie s'est levée` — reflexive direct object and feminine agreement.
5. `elle a sorti les chaises` — transitive sortir takes avoir.
6. `elles se sont parlé` — se is indirect, so parlé does not agree.
:::

## B. Choose the viewpoint

Translate and explain.
1. We worked for two hours, then we left. (mixed group)
2. I was tired when the lesson started. (male speaker)
3. The letters that she wrote are here.
4. Did you not understand the question?

:::answer
1. `Nous avons travaillé pendant deux heures, puis nous sommes partis.` — both activities are bounded events.
2. `J'étais fatigué quand la leçon a commencé.` — state as background, start as event.
3. `Les lettres qu'elle a écrites sont ici.` — preceding feminine plural direct object causes agreement.
4. `Est-ce que tu n'as pas compris la question ?` — ne…pas encloses the auxiliary.
:::

## C. A completed morning

Complete the account using passé composé: Hier, Julie (se réveiller) tôt. Elle (se laver) les mains. Elle (prendre) un café, puis elle (aller) au marché.

:::answer
`Hier, Julie s'est réveillée tôt. Elle s'est lavé les mains. Elle a pris un café, puis elle est allée au marché.` — distinguish reflexive direct-object agreement from a following body-part object; prendre uses avoir and aller uses être.
:::

## D. Independent event account

Write four sentences about yesterday. Include one avoir verb, one être verb with visible agreement, one negative, and one question. State your speaker's gender if relevant.

:::answer
Model, female speaker: `J'ai acheté des poires. Je suis rentrée à midi. Je n'ai pas préparé de dessert. Est-ce que tu as mangé les poires ?` — agreement appears in rentrée; ne…pas surrounds ai; the question keeps auxiliary + participle. Check a completed-event meaning in every sentence.
:::
