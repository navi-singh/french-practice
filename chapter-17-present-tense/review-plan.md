# Chapter 17 Seven-Day Review Plan

## Day 1

Study the first formation unit in lessons.md. Recall the model forms aloud, then attempt its checkpoint and translation rows 1–10.

## Day 2

Recall yesterday's forms without notes. Study the next unit, complete exercise Section A, and translate rows 11–25.

## Day 3

Study the remaining meaning and comparison units. Attempt their checkpoints and rows 26–40; explain every time or mood cue.

## Day 4

Complete Section B and rows 41–55. Record each error as a contrast between two possible forms, with a reason for rejecting one.

## Day 5

Complete Section C and rows 56–70. Read the corrected paragraph aloud twice, then retell it without looking.

## Day 6

Complete Section D and rows 71–85. Dictate six lesson or table examples using the speakable French, then check verb endings, agreements, and pronoun order.

## Day 7

Translate rows 86–100, redo missed Section A–B items closed-book, and produce six new sentences. For literary forms, recognize and interpret rather than compose.

## Spaced reviews

Repeat a ten-minute closed-book check on Days 10, 14, and 30:

1. recall one full paradigm and the chapter's main choice rule;
2. retry three previously missed items;
3. translate five randomly selected table prompts;
4. produce two original contemporary sentences;
5. review only errors, then schedule the next recall.

Use answer-key.md and the inline blocks after recall, never during it.
