# Chapter 17 Exercises: The Present Tense

Work without notes; explain the form as well as the meaning. Reveal each answer block only after attempting its section.

## A. Conjugation drill

1. finir: nous
2. vendre: elle
3. manger: nous
4. acheter: ils
5. faire: vous
6. venir: elles

:::answer
1. `nous finissons` — regular -ir has -iss-.
2. `elle vend` — no extra third-person -re ending.
3. `nous mangeons` — e preserves the soft g.
4. `ils achètent` — the stem vowel changes.
5. `vous faites` — irregular, not a regular -ez form.
6. `elles viennent` — doubled n in this stem.
:::

## B. Choose from meaning

Translate and explain.
1. I am reading a letter now.
2. She has been working here for three years.
3. We leave on Monday; the tickets are booked.
4. Do you (formal) know the answer?

:::answer
1. `Je lis une lettre maintenant.` — ordinary present includes progressive meaning.
2. `Elle travaille ici depuis trois ans.` — ongoing depuis takes the present.
3. `Nous partons lundi ; les billets sont réservés.` — fixed future arrangement in context.
4. `Est-ce que vous savez la réponse ?` — savoir for a known fact; est-ce que preserves normal verb order.
:::

## C. Context paragraph

Complete with the present of the verbs in parentheses:
Every morning: Nous (commencer) à neuf heures. Léa (prendre) le bus. Je (préférer) marcher. Aujourd'hui, nous (être) en avance.

:::answer
`Nous commençons à neuf heures. Léa prend le bus. Je préfère marcher. Aujourd'hui, nous sommes en avance.` — present habit changes to a present state; spelling and irregular stems still agree with each subject.
:::

## D. Independent production

Write four sentences about a weekday: a habit, an action happening now, a modal + infinitive, and a scheduled future departure. Use at least one irregular verb.

:::answer
Model: `Je prends un café chaque matin. En ce moment, je prépare mon sac. Je dois acheter un billet. Mon train part demain à sept heures.` — prends is a habit, prépare is current, dois governs an infinitive, and the last present has explicit future scheduling. Check all four meanings, not merely four present endings.
:::
