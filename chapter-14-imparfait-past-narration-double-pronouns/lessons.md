# Chapter 14 Lessons: The Imparfait, Past Narration, and Double Object Pronouns

Use short sessions. Accuracy from memory matters more than copying volume.

## Unit 1: Forming the imparfait

Take the present nous form, remove -ons, and add -ais, -ais, -ait, -ions, -iez, -aient. Être uses ét-.

### Examples

- `je parlais` — I was speaking/used to speak
- `nous finissions` — we were finishing/used to finish
- `vous étiez` — you were

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 2: Choosing the past tense

Use the imparfait for background, description, habit, and ongoing action; use the passé composé for bounded completed events.

### Examples

- `Il pleuvait.` — It was raining.
- `Nous habitions à Lyon.` — We lived/used to live in Lyon.
- `Soudain, le téléphone a sonné.` — Suddenly, the telephone rang.

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 3: Past narration

Set the scene with the imparfait and move events forward with the passé composé.

### Examples

- `Je dormais quand Marie a appelé.` — I was sleeping when Marie called.
- `Il faisait beau, puis un orage a commencé.` — The weather was nice, then a storm began.

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 4: Double object pronouns

Pronoun order is fixed before ordinary verbs. With affirmative commands, pronouns follow the verb in a different order.

### Examples

- `Je le lui donne.` — I give it to him/her.
- `Nous y en mettons.` — We put some there.
- `Donnez-le-moi !` — Give it to me!

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Chapter production task

Write a short travel memory: four background sentences in the imparfait and four completed events in the passé composé.

After writing, circle conjugated verbs, underline agreement or pronoun choices, and read the passage aloud twice.

## Sentence usage practice

Use these complete sentences to practice the chapter structures in context. Read each sentence aloud twice, cover it, and write it from memory.

1. `Il pleuvait et les rues étaient presque vides.` — It was raining and the streets were almost empty.
2. `Je lisais dans ma chambre quand le téléphone a sonné.` — I was reading in my room when the telephone rang.
3. `Nous habitions à Paris lorsque nous avons rencontré Léa.` — We were living in Paris when we met Léa.
4. `Paul voulait les billets, alors je les lui ai donnés.` — Paul wanted the tickets, so I gave them to him.

### Notice and mark

For each sentence, circle the target verb or grammar structure. Underline the word or phrase that controls its form.

### Change the sentence

Rewrite every sentence twice:

1. change the subject, person, or number;
2. change one meaningful detail such as the place, object, time, or description.

Make every required change to conjugation, agreement, articles, pronouns, and word order.

### Make it personal

Write four new sentences about your own life using the same four chapter structures. Read them aloud, check them, and rewrite only the parts you corrected.
