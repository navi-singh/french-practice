# Chapter 23: Imperative and Practical Non-finite Forms

This original, standalone learning pack reteaches the forms before asking you to choose them in context. Examples use standard France French.

## Outcomes

- Give positive and negative instructions with accurate pronoun order.
- Form present participles and gérondifs without treating them as finite tenses.
- Select infinitives after modals, prepositions, and common verb patterns.
- Distinguish verbal participles from agreeing adjectives.

## Lesson sequence

Follow the units in [lessons.md](lessons.md), then complete [exercises.md](exercises.md). The last lesson section contains 100 English-to-French sentences: rows 1–70 emphasize this chapter's objective, and rows 71–100 mix earlier learning. In Chapters 24–25, contemporary production and literary recognition stay separate.

## Recommended workflow

1. Read one unit and say its French examples aloud.
2. Attempt the unit's checkpoint before opening its inline answer.
3. Complete exercise Sections A–D without notes.
4. Translate ten table rows per session; reveal both answer and cue only after trying.
5. Consult [answer-key.md](answer-key.md) and record the reason for each correction.
6. Follow [review-plan.md](review-plan.md), including delayed recall.

## Mastery standard

Aim for 85% on a fresh attempt. Explain each tense or mood choice, then produce six different examples without copying. A correct alternative translation is acceptable when it preserves the requested meaning and time relationship.
