# Chapter 23 Exercises: Imperative and Practical Non-finite Forms

Work without notes; explain the form as well as the meaning. Reveal each answer block only after attempting its section.

## A. Command and non-finite drill

1. parler: tu imperative
2. être: vous imperative
3. avoir: tu imperative
4. prendre: gérondif
5. savoir: present participle
6. finir: negative infinitive

:::answer
1. `parle` — -er imperative drops tu -s.
2. `soyez` — irregular imperative.
3. `aie` — irregular imperative.
4. `en prenant` — nous prenons stem + -ant, preceded by en.
5. `sachant` — irregular present participle.
6. `ne pas finir` — both negative words precede the infinitive.
:::

## B. Pronouns and constructions

Translate and explain.
1. Give it to me. (it = the book)
2. Do not give it to me. (same book)
3. Let us get up.
4. I learn by listening.
5. She is trying not to fall.
6. After eating, we went out. (mixed group)

:::answer
1. `Donne-le-moi.` — affirmative: verb + direct object + moi.
2. `Ne me le donne pas.` — negative: ordinary preverbal order.
3. `Levons-nous.` — affirmative reflexive pronoun follows.
4. `J'apprends en écoutant.` — gérondif of same-subject means.
5. `Elle essaie de ne pas tomber.` — essayer de and negated infinitive.
6. `Après avoir mangé, nous sommes sortis.` — past infinitive for the earlier action.
:::

## C. Practical instruction sequence

Translate: Wash your hands before cooking. Cut the vegetables while listening to the radio. Put them in the pan. Do not add too much salt. Use informal singular commands.

:::answer
`Lave-toi les mains avant de cuisiner. Coupe les légumes en écoutant la radio. Mets-les dans la casserole. N'ajoute pas trop de sel.` — affirmative reflexive and object pronouns follow the verb; the negative command has no attached pronoun; avant de and en introduce non-finite forms with the same implied subject.
:::

## D. Independent practical directions

Write four instructions for a visitor: one affirmative with two pronouns, its negative counterpart, a gérondif with a clear shared subject, and an infinitive after a governing expression.

:::answer
Model: `Donne-la-lui. Ne la lui donne pas encore. Regarde le plan en marchant. Essaie de trouver la rue principale.` — la can represent a key and lui its recipient; polarity changes pronoun placement. In sentence three, the addressee both looks and walks. In sentence four, essayer requires de, and trouver remains infinitive.
:::
