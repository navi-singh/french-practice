# Chapter 6 Exercises

Complete these without notes. Put a question mark beside uncertain answers.

## A. Transform or translate

1. I am going to travel.
2. to Canada
3. from France
4. to the United States
5. nous / faire
6. The weather is nice.

## B. Controlled production

1. Write a new sentence demonstrating **aller and the near future**. Use `Je vais partir.` only as a structural model; change its content.
2. Write a new sentence demonstrating **à and de**. Use `au marché` only as a structural model; change its content.
3. Write a new sentence demonstrating **geographical names**. Use `en France` only as a structural model; change its content.
4. Write a new sentence demonstrating **faire**. Use `Il fait froid.` only as a structural model; change its content.

## C. Four-way variation

Choose one sentence from each lesson unit and rewrite it four ways:

1. change the subject;
2. make it negative;
3. turn it into a question;
4. change one meaningful detail.

## D. Dictation and correction

Use text-to-speech to hear six examples from `lessons.md`. Write what you hear without looking. Correct in separate passes:

1. vocabulary;
2. verb form;
3. agreement or pronoun placement;
4. spelling and accents.

## E. Personal writing

Plan a four-day trip. State destinations, origins, weather, and at least four near-future activities.

## F. Oral recall

Explain each of the chapter's four main rules aloud in simple English, then give one French example from memory.
