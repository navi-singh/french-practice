# Chapter 6 Lessons: Aller, the Near Future, Prepositions, and Faire

Use short sessions. Accuracy from memory matters more than copying volume.

## Unit 1: Aller and the near future

Aller is irregular: vais, vas, va, allons, allez, vont. Follow it with an infinitive to say what is going to happen.

### Examples

- `Je vais partir.` — I am going to leave.
- `Nous allons visiter Québec.` — We are going to visit Quebec City.
- `Ils vont étudier.` — They are going to study.

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 2: À and de

À often marks destination or location; de often marks origin, possession, or source. Both contract with le and les.

### Examples

- `au marché` — to the market
- `aux États-Unis` — to/in the United States
- `du Canada` — from Canada
- `des magasins` — from the stores

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 3: Geographical names

Use en with many feminine countries and masculine countries beginning with a vowel, au with many masculine countries, and aux with plural country names.

### Examples

- `en France` — in/to France
- `au Japon` — in/to Japan
- `aux Pays-Bas` — in/to the Netherlands
- `du Mexique` — from Mexico

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 4: Faire

Faire forms: fais, fais, fait, faisons, faites, font. It appears in weather, activities, and many fixed expressions.

### Examples

- `Il fait froid.` — It is cold.
- `Je fais du vélo.` — I ride a bicycle.
- `Nous faisons attention.` — We are being careful.

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Chapter production task

Plan a four-day trip. State destinations, origins, weather, and at least four near-future activities.

After writing, circle conjugated verbs, underline agreement or pronoun choices, and read the passage aloud twice.

## Sentence usage practice

Use these complete sentences to practice the chapter structures in context. Read each sentence aloud twice, cover it, and write it from memory.

1. `Demain, nous allons visiter un village près de Québec.` — Tomorrow, we are going to visit a village near Quebec City.
2. `Elle vient de France, mais elle habite au Canada.` — She comes from France, but she lives in Canada.
3. `Il fait froid, alors je vais mettre mon manteau.` — It is cold, so I am going to put on my coat.
4. `À quelle heure allez-vous partir pour l'aéroport ?` — What time are you going to leave for the airport?

### Notice and mark

For each sentence, circle the target verb or grammar structure. Underline the word or phrase that controls its form.

### Change the sentence

Rewrite every sentence twice:

1. change the subject, person, or number;
2. change one meaningful detail such as the place, object, time, or description.

Make every required change to conjugation, agreement, articles, pronouns, and word order.

### Make it personal

Write four new sentences about your own life using the same four chapter structures. Read them aloud, check them, and rewrite only the parts you corrected.
