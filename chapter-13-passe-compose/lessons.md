# Chapter 13 Lessons: The Passé Composé

Use short sessions. Accuracy from memory matters more than copying volume.

## Unit 1: Avoir + past participle

Most verbs use present-tense avoir plus a past participle. Regular endings are -é for -er, -i for regular -ir, and -u for regular -re.

### Examples

- `J'ai parlé.` — I spoke.
- `Nous avons fini.` — We finished.
- `Elle a vendu.` — She sold.

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 2: Irregular participles

Learn frequent forms as verb chunks: faire/fait, prendre/pris, voir/vu, lire/lu, écrire/écrit, boire/bu, avoir/eu, être/été.

### Examples

- `Il a fait du sport.` — He exercised.
- `J'ai lu le message.` — I read the message.
- `Elles ont pris le train.` — They took the train.

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 3: Être verbs

A smaller movement/change group and pronominal verbs use être. The participle generally agrees with the subject.

### Examples

- `Elle est arrivée.` — She arrived.
- `Ils sont partis.` — They left.
- `Nous nous sommes levés.` — We got up.

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Unit 4: Placement and agreement

Negation surrounds the auxiliary. Short adverbs often follow it. A preceding direct-object pronoun may trigger agreement with an avoir verb.

### Examples

- `Je n'ai pas mangé.` — I did not eat.
- `Elle a bien travaillé.` — She worked well.
- `Les lettres ? Je les ai écrites.` — The letters? I wrote them.

### Active writing

1. Copy each example once while saying it.
2. Cover the examples.
3. Rewrite all three from memory.
4. Change the subject, noun, or time expression to make three new sentences.
5. Read your new sentences aloud.

## Chapter production task

Write a ten-sentence account of yesterday using six avoir verbs, two être verbs, one pronominal verb, and one negative.

After writing, circle conjugated verbs, underline agreement or pronoun choices, and read the passage aloud twice.

## Sentence usage practice

Use these complete sentences to practice the chapter structures in context. Read each sentence aloud twice, cover it, and write it from memory.

1. `Hier, j'ai préparé le dîner et j'ai regardé un film.` — Yesterday, I prepared dinner and watched a movie.
2. `Marie est arrivée à huit heures et elle est repartie à minuit.` — Marie arrived at eight and left again at midnight.
3. `Nous ne nous sommes pas levés tôt ce matin.` — We did not get up early this morning.
4. `Les photos ? Je les ai prises pendant le voyage.` — The photos? I took them during the trip.

### Notice and mark

For each sentence, circle the target verb or grammar structure. Underline the word or phrase that controls its form.

### Change the sentence

Rewrite every sentence twice:

1. change the subject, person, or number;
2. change one meaningful detail such as the place, object, time, or description.

Make every required change to conjugation, agreement, articles, pronouns, and word order.

### Make it personal

Write four new sentences about your own life using the same four chapter structures. Read them aloud, check them, and rewrite only the parts you corrected.
