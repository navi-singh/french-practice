## Sections C-F

These are productive tasks. Check them using this rule summary:

- **Avoir + past participle:** Most verbs use present-tense avoir plus a past participle. Regular endings are -é for -er, -i for regular -ir, and -u for regular -re.
- **Irregular participles:** Learn frequent forms as verb chunks: faire/fait, prendre/pris, voir/vu, lire/lu, écrire/écrit, boire/bu, avoir/eu, être/été.
- **Être verbs:** A smaller movement/change group and pronominal verbs use être. The participle generally agrees with the subject.
- **Placement and agreement:** Negation surrounds the auxiliary. Short adverbs often follow it. A preceding direct-object pronoun may trigger agreement with an avoir verb.

## Self-scoring

Award one point for each Section A answer and two points for each structurally correct Section B sentence.

- **85-100%:** proceed and schedule delayed review.
- **70-84%:** review only missed rules and retry the next day.
- **Below 70%:** repeat the lesson in two smaller sessions.

A corrected answer is not mastered until you can produce a different correct example on a later day.
